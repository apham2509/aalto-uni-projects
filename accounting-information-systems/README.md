# Hands-On Analytics on Accounting Information Systems (ABL-C1102)

Aalto University · 6 ECTS · Spring 2025 · Group case work · Grade: 5 (Excellent)

## Case: selecting a cloud-based accounting information system

Business case on outsourcing and accounting system selection for a small pharmacy: comparing three cloud-based accounting information systems on pricing models, transaction costs, integrated accounting services, data security, and scalability, plus analysis of which financial-administration processes to outsource.
