# Capstone: Business Intelligence (57E00500)

Aalto University · 6 ECTS · Spring 2024 · Group projects · Grade: 5 (Excellent)

Three BI deliverables covering visualization, dashboarding, and applied machine learning:

## 1. E-commerce insights — Wish.com summer fashion

Analysis of summer-fashion e-commerce listings: sales drivers, customer sentiment, and merchandising strategy recommendations.
→ `wish_ecommerce_insights.pdf`

## 2. Machine learning on hotel reviews — Weka

Predicting hotel ratings and service attributes from TripAdvisor review texts using Weka: model comparison and evaluation for customer-sentiment insight.
→ `weka_hotel_sentiment_ml.pdf`

## 3. UNICEF analytics challenge — Tableau

Visual analytics on UNICEF data around the UN Sustainable Development Goals (child mortality, poverty, education), built as a Tableau story.
→ `tableau_unicef_challenge.pdf`, `video_presentation.mp4`

**Tools:** Tableau, Weka, data storytelling
