# Capstone: Future-proofing Supply Chains (ISM-E3002)

Aalto University · 6 ECTS · Spring 2026 · Group project · Grade: 4 (Very Good)

## ABB case study - reducing lead-time variability in ABB Drives Services

Company case for ABB Drives Services: order complexity and fluctuating demand create variation in lead times, workload levels, and capacity utilization, making short-term planning and delivery predictability difficult. We analyzed order intake and workload data (2023-2025) to identify the key drivers behind lead-time variation, backlog build-up, and capacity pressure, and proposed planning improvements.
